05 / CONCLUSION & METHODOLOGY

What can we conclude?

The average rating is approximately 4.00 and the median is 4.02, suggesting generally positive ratings in this collection.

Four- and five-star ratings account for the largest portions of the prepared rating summary.

James Patterson and Stephen King have the highest book counts in the author-count table used for this project.

These findings describe this dataset, not every book or reader. Dataset selection, missing records, and how authors or editions are counted can affect the results.

Data and tools

The data was obtained from Goodreads. The project organises the data and presents descriptive statistics and charts using spreadsheets and web tools.

Goodreads is the stated data source; this student project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Goodreads.
